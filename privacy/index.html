Your privacy.

This website does not store your inquiry answers. The inquiry form opens a draft in your email app, and you choose whether to send it to Jesse at jesse@jessehall.com.

Email inquiries

When you send an inquiry, we receive the information you include and use it to respond about your business and potential services. Do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Website services

The production website is intended for GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process request information to operate the service. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives requests from your browser. This website has no site analytics or email signup service.

Introduction video

The introduction uses YouTube’s privacy-enhanced embedded player. The homepage hero player loads when it comes into view, and the video starts when you press Play. The About section’s video thumbnail is hosted with this website and loads YouTube only when you choose to watch. Once loaded, YouTube may process information about your device, connection, and viewing activity under Google’s Privacy Policy.
