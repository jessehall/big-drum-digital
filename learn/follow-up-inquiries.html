TODO TODAY 08 / More inquiries

Follow up while the inquiry is still fresh

Make sure interested customers hear back from a real person.

Choose a responsible person

Decide who checks new inquiries and where they arrive. A shared inbox is useful only if someone owns the follow-up.

Write a helpful first response

Thank the person, acknowledge their question, and offer a specific next step. If you need more information, keep the request short.

Make a routine you can keep

Choose a response window that fits your business. Check the inbox consistently and track inquiries that still need an answer. Respect requests to stop contacting someone.

Your one thing today

Review your last five inquiries. Reply to any waiting for an answer, then decide who checks new inquiries each day.
