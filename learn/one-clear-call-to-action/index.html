TODO TODAY 07 / Website

Give your website one clear next step

Replace vague buttons with an action your visitor understands.

Choose what matters

Pick the main action that helps a customer move forward: request an estimate, book a session, or call your team.

Use clear button text

“Request an estimate” tells people more than “Submit” or “Get started.” Add a short note explaining what happens next.

Follow the entire path

Click the button yourself. Complete the form or booking flow. Check that the inquiry reaches the right person and the visitor receives a useful confirmation.

Your one thing today

Find the most important button on your homepage. Make its label specific and test the full journey.
