TODO TODAY 10 / Planning

Choose a business goal for the next 90 days

Make your next marketing decision easier with one focused goal.

Choose an outcome

Pick something connected to your business, such as qualified inquiries or booked appointments. Website visits can support the goal, but they are not the same as a new customer.

Know your starting point

Count the outcome over a recent, representative period. Write down where the numbers came from so you can compare fairly later.

Pick a practical next step

Set a realistic target and choose one action you believe could help. Review progress regularly and adjust the plan as you learn.

Your one thing today

Write: “Over the next 90 days, I want to move [outcome] from [starting point] to [target]. My first action is [step].”

Want a hand putting this to work?

Tell us what you want to accomplish. We’ll help you choose the next step.
