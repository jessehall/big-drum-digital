TODO TODAY 05 / Get found

Ask for a review without making it awkward

Make a genuine review easy for a happy customer.

Ask at the right moment

After completing a job and confirming the customer is satisfied, thank them and ask if they would share their experience.

Keep the request simple

Try: “Thanks for choosing us. If you have a minute, would you share an honest review of your experience? Here’s the link.” Use the review link supplied by your business listing.

Respect the customer

Do not pressure people, write their review, offer rewards, or ask only for a particular rating. Check the current rules of the review platform before setting up a campaign.
